Last updated: November 2026

ClipPost is a personal tool operated by [YOUR NAME] to publish the owner's own video clips to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

What access it requests

ClipPost asks for permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel (the YouTube upload scope) and to publish content to the owner's Instagram professional account. It does not read, change, or delete any other data in those accounts.

What data is stored

Data is not sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone. Videos and captions are sent only to YouTube and Instagram, as the owner directs, in order to publish them.

Google API Services

ClipPost's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions for Google, and in Instagram's Apps and Websites settings.
